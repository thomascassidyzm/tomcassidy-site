## 0. The two findings that change the shape of the plan

### 0.1 The Grade 11 AP pair is riskier than the ACS plan assumes

"AP Business with Personal Finance" is a real College Board course and a real exam — the ACS
course name maps exactly. It sits inside the new **AP Career Kickstart** programme and launches
in the **2026-27 school year**, with a fully digital Bluebook exam planned for **May 2027**
([AP Central](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-business-personal-finance),
[AP Students](https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-career-kickstart)).

So the exam exists. Two caveats make it the weakest link in the Grade 11 year:

1. **Coursework is welded to the exam.** One of the free-response questions is an exam-day
   validation question about the student's **Business Canvas Project**
   ([exam overview](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-business-personal-finance/exam-overview)).
   This is not a sit-and-pass exam a private candidate can walk into cold — it presumes a taught
   course with a project artefact behind it. Whatever teaching arrangement Ashton has must
   produce that project, and it must be his.
2. **It is a debut-year course.** Which schools and centres will actually be authorised to offer
   and order it in year one is unclear. The College Board's own adoption page carries no
   application process, no deadline and no eligibility detail — only an interest form
   ([adopt page](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-business-personal-finance/adopt)).
   For an external candidate needing someone else to order the exam, a brand-new course is the
   hardest case, not the easiest.

**GAP, and it is the single most important unresolved item in this document:** whether an
external / home-educated candidate can sit AP Business with Personal Finance in May 2027 at all,
and specifically whether the Prometric international centres (section 2) will carry it in its
first year. AP Homeschoolers, which runs online AP courses for home-educated students, lists the
course but records the exam date as "TBD"
([aphomeschoolers.com](https://www.aphomeschoolers.com/course/apbusiness/)) — which is itself a
signal that the homeschool route for this specific exam is not yet settled.

**This is a question for the dad and the ACS counsellor, this week, not a detail to discover in
March.** If the answer is no, or unclear by around October 2026, the fallback is to swap an
established AP into Grade 11 in its place. That should be a deliberate decision taken early,
with the ACS counsellor's view on record, rather than a scramble.

### 0.2 AP exam access is probably NOT a reason to choose one provider over another

This was expected to be a constraint driving the provider choice. On the evidence it is not.

AP has partnered with **Prometric** to administer AP exams in both countries Ashton will be
living in, and Prometric's own UK page states that all students are welcome to test
"regardless of their country of residence or school they attend"
([Prometric UK](https://www.prometric.com/exams/cbapuk/),
[Prometric Turkey](https://www.prometric.com/exams/cbaptr/)). College Board confirms that
secondary-level students who are homeschooled, in independent study, or at virtual schools are
eligible to test at authorised centres in both territories
([Register for AP Exams in the UK](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/register-for-ap-exams-in-the-uk.pdf),
[Register for AP Exams in Turkey](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/register-for-ap-exams-in-turkey.pdf)).

- **UK centre:** Escalla, 55 Broadway, London SW1H 0BD — on top of St James's Park tube. Roughly
  an hour from Woking.
- **Turkey:** administered via Prometric's Istanbul arrangements; students register and pay
  Prometric directly to reserve a seat.
- **ID:** an original, valid, non-expired **passport** is required for entry at authorised
  centres in both countries. Worth saying out loud because a passport expiring in spring 2027
  would quietly destroy the exam sitting.

There is a second, older route that the College Board also documents: a homeschooled student
cannot order exams directly, so a school's AP coordinator adds them to an exam-only section
([AP Central, students from other schools](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/exam-administration-ordering-scores/ordering-fees/ordering-exam-materials/special-ordering-circumstances/students-other-study)).
On that route the 2026-27 dates are **13 November 2026** and **12 March 2027**, the latter also
being the point at which a coordinator can have the late-order fee waived for exactly this
category of student.

**What this means for the meeting:** the provider decision can be judged almost entirely on the
credential rail — accreditation, whether externally-delivered work is accepted and who assigns
the grade, how the ACS Grades 9-10 credits land, any residency requirement, and the Common App
school report. "Do they run AP exams" is close to a non-question, because the Prometric route
exists in both of Ashton's cities independently of any school.

**GAP:** exact per-exam fee, the centre's own registration fee, and the registration window for
the London and Istanbul Prometric centres. Prometric states these are set by individual centres,
and advance registration by the November before the testing year is recommended. The published
figures are being chased and will be in the next revision with the source against each. No number
is stated here rather than a plausible one.
