> First rough-but-honest assembly of Tom's coaching method from every telling found across the
> estate, 2026-07-14. Built for Tom to redline, not to be believed. His words are quoted verbatim
> with pointers; connective tissue and interpretive calls are marked [ASSEMBLER]. The most valuable
> section is §3 (contradictions and drift) — that is the editing queue.
>

> single canonical home" for the coaching voice (commit 82e1dcc). This document does not compete
> with it — it audits it against the whole estate, records where the other tellings agree, differ,
> or carry moves the canonical file dropped, and indexes every file where the method lives.
>
> Relationship to `docs/REASON-ABILITY-CANON.md` (2026-08-04): that document covers the whole
> Reason-Ability system — the four dogmas, the minimum-will week, the no-civil-war doctrine,
> future-anticipation, the name — built on Tom's spoken talk-through. This document covers the
> Script and the Sausage Machine engine, which is the inside of each of that system's weeks.

## 1. The method, stated once

Assembled from Tom's own words. Each block cites where the wording comes from.

### The two mistakes, and the shape of the whole thing

> "Most people only make 2 mistakes in life: 1. They don't THINK properly about what they actually
> want out of life. 2. They don't TAKE ACTION to do something about it."
> — `src/content/essays/reason-ability-an-overview.md`

> "success = reason x reasonable … you have to have a reason for doing anything, and what you do
> has to then be reasonable." — same file

[ASSEMBLER] Everything below slots into that pair. The Script work fixes the thinking mistake; the
Sausage Machine and the 13x4 fix the action mistake. The two meta-questions are the whole method in
miniature, and Tom has already paired them himself:

> "The meta-questions apply to everything: What would you choose? (Reason - thinking properly).
> What are you putting in? (Reasonable - taking action)."
> — `src/lib/programs/conquering-life.ts:62`

### The Script

The fullest telling in Tom's own prose is `src/content/essays/reasonable-doubt.md`:

> "We say you're absolutely correct, something else IS choosing for you and that something else is
> called your script. Well, we call it your script. We say that 'everything that isn't helpful,
> that you wouldn't choose, that doesn't serve you' is not you. It's your script." (lines 125–127)

> "If we WOULD choose to have a different thought, it can only mean that we're NOT CHOOSING our
> thought." (line 119)

> "If what you are thinking/feeling is what you WOULD choose, then it's you. It must be. It's what
> you WOULD choose, so it's the authentic you. Genuinely behaving according to your intuition, your
> conscious beliefs. If however, what you're thinking/feeling is NOT what you WOULD choose, then
> it's your script." (lines 145–147)

The live 2014 voice (Reasonable Wealth WhatsApp, `~/reasonable-wealth-coaching-voice.md`):

> "Yasmin: it's not you. You would never CHOOSE that thinking. It's not helpful. … All it is, is
> the voice in your head that is making your choices for you." (line 22)

> "So it tries to protect you from going for stuff, because it thinks you can't handle the pain of
> failure. No! You can easily handle the pain of failure. When you are out of your script, there is
> no pain of failure." (lines 447–450)

> "It's just your script. Your programming. Your past experiences talking. It's not YOU. It's not
> your fault. … You don't need to change. You are perfect as you are." (lines 381–387, 444–445)

### The coaching flow

As crystallised in the Thrive engine (tom-system-prompt.md §"YOUR CONVERSATION FLOW") and condensed
in `the-script.md:98`:

> Listen → Gateway Question → reveal the Script → clarify what they actually want (keep going until
> it's crystal clear) → apply Sausage-Machine thinking → support their conscious choice.

With the delivery discipline: Socratic, one thing per response — "a question, OR one brief
explanation, OR one observation — then stop and wait" (the-script.md:23–26; tom-system-prompt.md:9–13).

### The cadence: 13×4 (and 9×4)

> "We use the 13x4. Any goal you want to accomplish is broken down into 13 component parts. Each
> week you focus on just one of these components at a time, leaving all others to their natural
> chance. After 13 weeks you repeat the cycle. The cycle repeats 4 times per year, since 13x4=52.
> … MAINTAIN focus. CHANGE routines." — `src/content/essays/reason-ability-an-overview.md:131–135`

Lineage, from the teaching variant:

> "It was first turned into a system by Benjamin Franklin who used it to master the skills he
> required to develop his character. It has often been referred to as the Franklin 13 … since he
> was working on the full 52 weeks in a year and 13x4 = 52." — `src/content/essays/the-9x4.md:23`
> (the 9×4 is the 36-week academic-year version: 9 characteristics × 4 cycles)

Why it works (the-9x4.md §"Why does it work so well?"): action, continuous, **focus** not effort,
low floor / high ceiling, minimum administration. The wheel arithmetic is implemented once in
`src/lib/coach-engine.ts` ("the wheel and the push must never disagree about which focus it is"),
and the Script and Sausage Machine themselves appear as weekly focuses on the Reasonable Leadership
wheel (`src/lib/programs/reasonable-leadership.ts:84–97`).

## 3. Contradictions and drift — the editing queue

Ranked by how much they matter, each one line of decision for Tom.

**3.1 Become/Do/GIVE vs Become/Do/HAVE.** the-script.md:60–63 and tom-system-prompt.md:86–89 say
GIVE, explicitly arguing "NOT what you want to have. Having is a RESULT of giving." But
reason-ability-an-overview.md:71–75 (his own draft essay, front-page framework) says "**Become** ·
**Do** · **Have** — what you have created to surround you". One of these is stale. [ASSEMBLER] My
read: GIVE is the considered position (it has the argument attached); the overview essay predates
or drifted. Needs his ruling before the essay ships un-draft.

**3.2 The provenance gap: Richard Wilkins / Broadband Consciousness.** the-script.md presents the
Script and the Sausage Machine with no mention of their source. But Tom's own essay says "For those
of you who want to know more about the script, please check out my personal mentor's website,
Richard Wilkins" (actors-agents-architects.md:87), and the Thrive repo carries a full BC
methodology file (`context/bc-methodology.md`) whose Script and Sausage Machine definitions are
near-identical to Tom's, plus a whole Richard & Liz coaching platform (`~/richard-liz-coaching`).
And the primary source is unambiguous — Tom, RW1, 03/03/2014: "Forever indebted to my personal
mentor, the great Richard Wilkins for coming up with that metaphor..." (said of the sausage
machine, RW1.txt:2222). The Thrive Wiz persona file goes further, labelling both frameworks
Wilkins' own: "THE SCRIPT FRAMEWORK (Your Creation)" and "THE SAUSAGE MACHINE (Your Creation)"
(`~/thrive/thrive-website/coaching-prompts/wiz-system-prompt.md:85,166`). The canon has to decide
how the inheritance is acknowledged: what is BC's, what is Tom's layer on top.

Useful boundary markers — vocabulary that is BC/Wilkins-side and never appears in Tom's own
material: the Script Poem (wiz-system-prompt.md:88–108), "Scriptjacked", "SHIT-FM", "Script Aware",
"95% of the time, the Script is choosing" (wiz-system-prompt.md:110–125), and "Scriptitis — there's
only ONE disease" (bc-methodology.md:161). Liz Ivory's ingredient exercise (score an area 1–10,
list 5 ingredients totalling 100g, "would you choose these?", redesign — liz-system-prompt.md:200–256)
is her own operationalisation of the shared sausage-machine metaphor, also not Tom's. Anything
using this vocabulary is quoting the lineage, not Tom. [ASSEMBLER] Tom's distinctive layer looks like: Helpful>True as the discriminator, the
Reason-Ability pairing of the two meta-questions, Become/Do/Give, Choose easy, and the 13x4 as the
implementation engine. The Script/Sausage Machine core is inherited and openly credited elsewhere —
the-script.md just never says so.

**3.3 "Step 1: Notice. Step 2: There is no Step 2."** The deepest 2014 move, and the structural
heart of the Ultimate 13×4 (NOTICE as week-1 hub), is missing from the-script.md as a sentence.
Either it belongs in the canonical file's signature moves, or Tom considers it superseded by the
flow. One line from him settles it.

**3.4 "the Script" vs "your script".** Thrive and the-script.md capitalise it and drop the
possessive ("that's usually Script running", "trained in Script awareness frameworks"); the essays
and 2014 voice always say "your script", lowercase (reasonable-doubt.md:127 even says "Well, WE
call it your script"). [ASSEMBLER] Not cosmetic: "your script" keeps it personal inheritance,
"Script" makes it a universal mechanism. Which register is canonical?

**3.5 Which inner voice is the real you.** reasonable-leadership.ts:88 frames the test as "whether
the voice in your head is your intuition or the script". But 2014 has a stronger, stranger line:
"The voice that nags you is the real you. The other voice cannot be you choosing. Since it's not
helpful" (RW lines 248–250) — there the *nagging* voice (the dream that won't die) is you, and the
comfortable voice is script. [ASSEMBLER] These are reconcilable (both resolve via "is it helpful")
but the surface wording points opposite ways; worth one clarifying sentence in the canon.

**3.6 WHAT not HOW went missing.** "Your job is the WHAT. Let the HOW be downloaded to you" (RW
471–476) appears nowhere after 2014, yet it is load-bearing for the anti-agonising stance and pairs
naturally with intelligence-vs-intuition (RW 329–341). Deliberate retirement or accidental loss?

**3.7 How strong is Helpful > True?** Three strengths coexist: the coaching absolute ("There is no
truth — don't listen to anyone, don't listen to me", the-script.md:17); the amplified "Helpful >>
True" (distinction-physics axiom-arena:1451); and the epistemically careful Configuration-Economics
version — "Not because truth doesn't matter … We don't do anything we know to be false"
(NOTES-recursive-fitness-and-bridge-test.md:31). [ASSEMBLER] These serve different audiences
(coachee vs research programme) and probably should differ — but the canon should say that on
purpose rather than leave it as drift.

**3.8 Sausage machine scope.** Personal input→output coaching tool (Thrive) vs "our collective
thoughts as the universal sausage machine bringing anything we want for us, for everyone, for the
future of humanity" (architects-of-the-future.md:74) vs BC's "detection system" framing. Three
sizes of the same machine; canon should name the spectrum.

**3.9 Cadence naming.** 13x4 (general, Franklin 52-week) and 9x4 (teaching, 36-week academic year)
are both live, plus "the Ultimate 13x4" as a specific wheel. Fine as a family; the canon should
state the rule ("N×4: N focuses, four laps, sized to the year that contains them" [ASSEMBLER —
proposed wording, not Tom's]).

**3.10 The canonical file is wired to nothing.** `guide-prompt.ts:17–21` promises "Build any
coaching-mode behaviour FROM that file (import with `?raw`)" — but no such import exists anywhere;
the delegation is aspirational. The estate-scout memo already lists "wire the-script.md into the
live coach" as the pending move (`~/estate-scout/reasonability.md:44`). Voice-in-a-doc the engine
doesn't consume is drift waiting to happen — this is the mechanical fix that stops §3 growing.

**3.11 Which move-names are Tom's — now partly settled by source.** CONFIRMED Tom's own:
"Helpful > True" (verbatim, his thumbs, Architects 29/03/2014 22:44:59 — exactly that notation) and
"Choose easy" (flagged in `tom-coaching-verbose-backup.js` as "TOM'S ACTUAL RESPONSES FROM
TRANSCRIPTS ✓", though it appears once, without a framework around it). STILL UNCONFIRMED in any

1,637-line verbose backup — closest 2014 analogue is "not NOT done", which is DO-flavoured only)
and DING!/Duuuum/energy-vampires as named vocabulary. [ASSEMBLER] Become/Do/Give looks like a
later-stage formalisation — possibly Tom's own later coinage, possibly the Thrive distillation's.
Needs his word.

**3.12 Person drift.** Programme data consistently asks first-person "What would I choose?";
the-script.md and the essays ask second-person "What WOULD you choose?". Both are Tom's: RW1
03/03/2014 has him modelling the self-tool first-person — "What would I choose? What makes me feel
better now?" (RW1.txt:2211–2212). Coach-voice vs self-tool — probably both intended, but unstated.

**3.14 Noticing > Changing vs "re-programming the script".** Both verbatim Tom, one day apart in
the Architects group: "It's all about re-programming the script" (28/03/2014 17:55:49) and
"Noticing > Changing" — agreeing with Stephanie Sales's "Not just noticing, not having to 'kill'
the script" (29/03/2014 21:37:19–21:38:06, Architects #1.txt). RESOLVED BY THE SOURCE ITSELF an

## 4. Method vs persona — where the boundary runs

**The coaching persona is Tom himself, not Alexander.** The Thrive engine literally instructs "You
are Tom" (tom-system-prompt.md:323). The Script / Sausage Machine method is Tom's coaching voice.

**Alexander is a different job: the epistemic/teaching guide.** Across
`~/alexander/pedagogy-core/pedagogy-core.md`, `~/alexander/PEDAGOGY-DNA.md` and this repo's
`src/lib/guide-prompt.ts`, Alexander is the Socrates→Plato→Aristotle→Alexander chain: distinction
networks ("Understanding IS a network of precise distinctions"), same/different spiralling inward,
"dog, not dentist" energy, facts-vs-concepts calibration, "nowhere to hide — but with love". That
is pedagogy for understanding a corpus or a subject, not coaching for choosing a life.

**What the two share (the common spine):** Socratic drawing-out; one thing per response, then stop
and wait; "a distinction someone builds for themselves is one they keep"; warmth without
performance; British register. [ASSEMBLER] This shared spine is why the two get entangled in the
sources — e.g. `guide-prompt.ts` houses Alexander AND points to the-script.md for any coaching
mode ("Build any coaching-mode behaviour FROM that file… It is the single home for that voice"),
which is the right wiring: same discipline, different corpus, different goal.

**Confirmed negative (worker deep-read of the alexander repo, all 17 branches + history):** none of
the method's named terms — Script/Sausage Machine, Become/Do/Give, Choose easy, the Gateway
Question, Helpful > True — appear anywhere in that repo. One false cognate to guard against: the
old Dom persona (`api/coaching.js`, older branches) says "The Script they're running probably
sounds like…" and "Let's expose your Script" — a thin borrow of the word for a student's self-talk,
never the framework. Alexander's own method family is HEXAGON / SAME-DIFFERENT / UFAST / Teachback,
which was deliberately restructured into the "progressive layers" distinction-network model on
2025-10-28 (commits 245c50a, faad57a). Separate lineage, separate canon — it belongs to
`~/alexander/pedagogy-core/`, not to this document.

**Rule of thumb for future files** [ASSEMBLER — proposed]: if the move ends in the learner
understanding a thing, it's Alexander; if it ends in the person choosing consciously, it's the
Script method in Tom's voice. Neither should restate the other's content.
